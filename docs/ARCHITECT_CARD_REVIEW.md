## Cross-card differentiation

Van Duysen vs Zumthor:
- Van Duysen asks which views and materials to restrain; Zumthor asks how the body experiences sequence, material, sound, and light.

Zumthor vs Kahn:
- Zumthor focuses on atmosphere and sensory thresholds; Kahn focuses on spatial order, served/servant hierarchy, and structure as explanation.

Kahn vs Ando:
- Kahn starts from order, service hierarchy, and structure; Ando starts from geometry, walls, processional approach, and light/dark contrast.

Ando vs Tange:
- Ando works through wall, route, light, and framed nature at architectural scale; Tange expands the question to street, plaza, public flow, structure, and urban systems.

Tange vs Van Duysen:
- Tange asks how the proposal belongs to civic and urban systems; Van Duysen asks what to withhold, align, and selectively open within a restrained composition.
