# Jucho-kun x Esquisse-kun Workflow

[日本語](WORKFLOW_JUCHO_ESQUISSE.md)

## Purpose

Keep administrative research and drawing review separate so humans can verify assumptions against official sources.

```text
Administrative research with Jucho-kun
→ Human verification against official sources
→ Drawing review with Esquisse-kun
```

## Roles

| Step | Tool / Actor | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Administrative research | Jucho-kun | Organize candidate sources for zoning, BCR, FAR, fire districts, hazards, and related data |
| Official verification | Human | Check laws, local-government sources, GIS, and official documents |
| Drawing review | Esquisse-kun | Review design conditions, spatial planning, and missing code-screening information in drawings |

## Rules

- Agreement between AI outputs is not proof.
- Final decisions must be verified by humans against official sources.
- Esquisse-kun does not invent missing drawing values.
- Estimated values are not used as legal grounds.

## Recommended Inputs

- Jucho-kun research notes
- Official URLs
- Human-verified administrative information
- Site plans, plans, sections, and area schedules

## Output Use

Esquisse-kun output is preliminary review only. Do not use it as final proof for building confirmation, legal compliance, rights clearance, or design approval.
